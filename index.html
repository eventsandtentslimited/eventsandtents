Business Overview

A full-service events & equipment solutions company

Events & Tents Limited is an Abuja-based, full-service events, event production, management, logistics, and equipment solutions company serving clients across Nigeria, with the capacity to operate nationwide.

Our scope extends beyond conventional event planning to deliver end-to-end solutions, including event infrastructure, equipment rental, venue setup, technical production, logistics, hospitality support, vendor coordination, consultancy, and project management.

Our Services

From first concept to final guest, one accountable team for every layer of your event.

Event Planning & Management

Planning, coordination, execution, and management of events, with theme development, custom concepts and venue styling that match your vision.

Event Production

Technical and physical production covering staging, lighting, sound and event infrastructure, built to look and sound exceptional.

Equipment Rental

Hiring, leasing, supplying, installing, and maintaining event equipment, including our signature marquee tents.

Logistics & Operations

Consultancy & Project Mgmt.

Professional advisory, technical support, and project management for events of every scale and sector.

Marketing & Promotion
